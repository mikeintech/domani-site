The record started at a table. A few people, a deck of questions, nobody allowed to dodge. The game is simple: someone says something they've never done, and whoever has done it has to own it.

Domani wrote it about that moment across the table, when a hot question gets you closer to the person in front of you than a month of small talk ever could.
